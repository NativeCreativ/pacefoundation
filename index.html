Our Vision

To extend Pace Group’s core expertise in connecting and powering India into enduring community upliftment by fostering better learning environments, accessible healthcare, environmental sustainability, dignified elderly care, and responsive need-based community interventions through purposeful and sustainable initiatives.

Our Approach to CSR

At Pace Group, our approach centres on practical, community-focused interventions that address identified needs and create lasting impact. We strengthen government-school infrastructure to support quality education, enhance mobility and patient care through targeted healthcare initiatives, and work towards dignified living environments for senior citizens. We collaborate with schools, healthcare institutions, community organizations, and implementation partners to design and deliver solutions that respond to local priorities while emphasizing responsible implementation, regular project monitoring, and transparent reporting. Alongside these core focus areas, we remain responsive to eligible need-based community requests that align with our CSR objectives and are approved through the prescribed governance process.

Focus Themes

Delivering targeted, accountable, and sustainable interventions across communities.

Education

We work to strengthen educational infrastructure in underserved schools through classroom upgrades, sanitation improvements, student seating, and learning resources, helping create supportive environments where children can learn with confidence and dignity.

3 government schools in Bengaluru with following support

  • Classroom construction/renovation
  • Dual-seat benches
  • Dedicated sanitation and water blocks
  • Educational supplies
Impact: “Average 23% increase in admissions”

Our healthcare initiatives focus on enhancing mobility, strengthening patient care, and supporting the health and well-being of vulnerable children through partnerships with trusted community organizations.

1. Mobility support: 50 Wheelchairs Distributed

Supporting persons with locomotor disabilities through the provision of wheelchairs, enabling greater independence, mobility, and participation in daily life.

Next Phase: An additional 50 wheelchairs are planned for distribution during the current year.
2. Child Care and Health Support 26 Children Supported

Supporting children under residential care with medical assistance, nutrition, education, clothing, and essential day-to-day needs to promote their overall health, development, and well-being.

3. Patient Care Infrastructure Strengthening Patient Care

Supporting healthcare institutions with patient monitoring equipment to improve the quality of care available to underserved communities.

Recognizing the growing need for quality elder care, Pace Group is developing an initiative focused on providing secure living environments, healthcare support, and opportunities for social well-being for senior citizens. Planning is currently underway.

In addition to our core focus areas, we provide one-time support for eligible community needs that require timely assistance. These interventions are guided by identified requirements from schools, healthcare institutions, community organizations, and local stakeholders, and are evaluated through our established CSR process to ensure meaningful and responsible impact.

Pace Group’s shared CSR commitment is backed by clear accountability at each company level. Every participating company carries out its CSR activities under its own CSR Policy, approvals and legal obligations under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, Schedule VII and the CSR Rules, as applicable; a common group identity does not replace the responsibilities of individual companies. The Board of each company approves its CSR Policy and expenditure, oversees implementation and ensures the required reporting and disclosures. Where applicable, a CSR Committee supports the Board by recommending the CSR Policy, expenditure and Annual Action Plan and by monitoring approved initiatives; where a separate committee is not required, the Board discharges these functions. The internal CSR team provides day-to-day oversight, evaluating proposals, engaging implementation partners, tracking project milestones, conducting field reviews, and maintaining progress and other documentation for review by the relevant company’s governance bodies. Pace Foundation, which was established by Pace Group, acts as an implementation partner, alongside other eligible partners where appropriate. Responsibility for CSR commitments, expenditure and disclosures always stays with the individual company, and every project is attributed to that company and its implementing organisation.
