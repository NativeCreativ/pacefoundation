# Pace Foundation — CSR Portal & Landing Page

Official Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) landing page for **Pace Foundation** and the **Pace Group CSR Ecosystem**, operating under the statutory framework of Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Overview
This platform presents the collective CSR initiatives, statutory disclosures, governance framework, and verifiable on-ground transformations delivered across India by Pace Group entities.

### Participating Group Entities
- **Pace Digitek Limited** — *Integrated Infrastructure & Energy Solutions* (Lead Primary Contributor)
- **Lineage Power Private Limited** — *Telecom Power & Energy Storage Solutions* (Power Partner)
- **Pace Renewable Energies Private Limited** — *Clean Energy Partner*
- **Pace Foundation** — *Dedicated Philanthropic & Implementation Arm* (MCA CSR-1 Registered)

---

## Core Focus Areas & Deliverables
1. **Quality Education & School Infrastructure Upgrades**
   - Structural civil renovation, waterproofing, and exterior facade modernization.
   - Dual-seat ergonomic benches and modern classroom seating.
   - Dedicated water and hygienic sanitation blocks ensuring student dignity and health.
   - Key project locations: Gangasandra Government School, Ramohalli Government School, Doddabasti Government School.

2. **Healthcare & Mobility Assistance**
   - Distribution of high-grade mobility wheelchairs for persons with locomotor disabilities.
   - Institutional donation of critical care patient monitoring equipment.
   - Continued nutrition, medical care, and residential support for vulnerable children.

3. **Dignified Elderly Care**
   - Roadmap development for accessible senior-friendly community living, geriatric health monitoring, and social well-being programs.

4. **Responsive Need-Based Community Support**
   - Grassroots assistance for verified emergency school fixtures, clean drinking water filtration, and community welfare needs sanctioned through the CSR Committee governance process.

## Technical Stack & Architecture
- **HTML5**: Clean semantic markup, full ARIA accessibility, high-contrast typography.
- **CSS3**: Vanilla CSS with modern custom properties (CSS variables), CSS Grid, Flexbox, hardware-accelerated animations, and responsive breakpoints.
- **JavaScript**: Pure ES6+ vanilla JavaScript (zero external dependencies/frameworks), lightweight modals, interactive ecosystem diagram, and infinite photo marquee.
- **Performance**: High-efficiency optimized imagery, responsive layouts, fast page loads.
