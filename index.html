Our Vision

To extend Pace Group’s core expertise in connecting and powering India into enduring community upliftment by fostering better learning environments, accessible healthcare, environmental sustainability, dignified elderly care, and responsive need-based community interventions through purposeful and sustainable initiatives.

Our Approach to CSR

At Pace Group, our approach centres on practical, community-focused interventions that address identified needs and create lasting impact. We strengthen government-school infrastructure to support quality education, enhance mobility and patient care through targeted healthcare initiatives, and work towards dignified living environments for senior citizens. We collaborate with schools, healthcare institutions, community organizations, and implementation partners to design and deliver solutions that respond to local priorities while emphasizing responsible implementation, regular project monitoring, and transparent reporting. Alongside these core focus areas, we remain responsive to eligible need-based community requests that align with our CSR objectives and are approved through the prescribed governance process.

Lineage Power Private Limited

Telecom Power & Energy Storage Solutions

A dedicated manufacturing company specialising in telecom passive power and battery energy storage solutions. Lineage Power designs, engineers and manufactures high-availability DC power systems, hybrid platforms and containerised Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) for telecom operators, data centres, utilities and renewable energy projects. Combining advanced manufacturing capabilities, engineering expertise and integrated lifecycle services, the company delivers reliable power infrastructure solutions across India and select international markets.

Theme 01 • Education: School Infrastructure Development

3 government schools in Bengaluru with following support

We work to strengthen educational infrastructure in underserved schools through classroom upgrades, sanitation improvements, student seating, and learning resources, helping create supportive environments where children can learn with confidence and dignity.

Supported Institutions in Bengaluru, Karnataka:
Government Higher Primary School, Gangasandra Government Higher Primary School, Ramohalli Government Lower Primary School, Doddabasti
✓
Classroom construction/renovation: Structural wall plastering, weatherproof painting, electrical rewiring, and energy-efficient LED fixtures.
✓
Dual-seat benches: Durable, ergonomic student dual-bench seating replacing floor seating for comfortable classroom learning.
✓
Dedicated sanitation and water blocks: Hygienic boys and girls toilet blocks with continuous running water supply and sanitation handwash stations.
✓
Educational supplies: Stationery kits, notebooks, and essential learning materials provided to all enrolled students.
Impact: “Average 23% increase in admissions” Following the comprehensive handover of modernized school classrooms and seating infrastructure, supported schools recorded a verified average 23% increase in student admissions.

Elderly care: Dignified Living for Senior Citizens

Recognizing the growing need for quality elder care, Pace Group is developing an initiative focused on providing secure living environments, healthcare support, and opportunities for social well-being for senior citizens. Planning is currently underway.

Key Focus Areas Under Active Planning:
  • Secure Living Environments: Safe, senior-friendly infrastructure tailored with accessibility features and emergency safeguards.
  • Healthcare Support: Regular health monitoring, doctor consultations, geriatric assistance, and palliative support.
  • Social Well-Being & Engagement: Safe community spaces, recreational facilities, and nutritious meals to foster active, healthy living.
Current Phase: Planning & Feasibility

Project roadmap and implementation planning are currently underway by the Pace Group CSR team.

CSR Governance & Oversight

Operating under a multi-tiered statutory governance architecture in strict accordance with Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013 and CSR Rules, 2014.

Pace Group operates under a robust, multi-tiered governance structure in accordance with Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014. The framework ensures strict statutory compliance, fiduciary oversight, transparent project approval, regular monitoring, and credible impact delivery across all participating group entities.

Companies Act § 135(1)

Board Oversight

The Board of Directors of each group company holds ultimate fiduciary responsibility for approving the CSR Policy, sanctioning annual budgetary allocations, and reviewing statutory CSR reporting.
